## Methodology & Caveats

**How it was built.** Two parallel research passes (one per platform) mined published sources: "best travel TikTokers/Instagrammers" roundups, Forbes Top Creators lists, travel creator awards, itinerary/guide-focused creator roundups. Handles were sanity-checked against social search for active travel content. Sources per creator are recorded in the `source_urls` column of `influencers.csv` (every row has 1–2 source URLs; the table above links profile URLs instead — use the CSV for full provenance).

**Itinerary-heavy filter.** Selection preferred creators who publish trip plans, "X days in" guides, and day-by-day itineraries over pure photography accounts. A few big photography-led accounts remain for reach/variety (flagged by niche, easy to filter).

**Caveats (read before using this list):**
- Follower counts are *as stated by the source at its publication date* (roughly May 2025 – Sep 2026), NOT live counts. 27 of 100 rows read `unknown` where no source stated a count — numbers were never invented.
- Two handles are verbatim from published lists and look like possible typos (`@limsawkward`, `@marklharriosn`); re-verify before any outreach.
- Deliberate exclusions: brand/magazine accounts (e.g. @afarmag) and curator/repost aggregators were dropped — the list targets individual creators.
- Instagram pass dropped a candidate whose listicle spelling could not be verified ("Eamon & Bec" listed as `@eomonbec`) rather than risk a guessed handle.
- Niche labels are editorial (solo, adventure, budget, luxury, cultural, food, family, couples, points-miles, guides, itineraries, beach, cruise, roadtrip).
- This is research-grade input for the pilot (influencer identification → itinerary extraction), not a ranked "top 1000" leaderboard and not safe for paid partnerships without re-verification.
